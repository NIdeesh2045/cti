LAB 9.2 - SIMULATE A NETWORK PARTITION

Replica Set: rs0
Docker Network: lab08_default
Members: mongo1, mongo2, mongo3

Experiment 1: Disconnect mongo1
- mongo1 became unreachable.
- mongo2 remained PRIMARY.
- mongo3 remained SECONDARY.
- w:1 insert: SUCCESS
- w:majority insert: SUCCESS

Experiment 2: Disconnect mongo3
- Only mongo2 remained connected.
- MongoDB lost voting majority.
- mongo2 stepped down and became SECONDARY.
- w:1 write attempt: FAILED - not primary.
- w:majority was not executed because the shell command stopped after the first error.

Experiment 3: Recovery
- Reconnected mongo3.
- mongo2 became PRIMARY again.
- Reconnected mongo1.
- All three data-bearing members became healthy.

Final Status:
- mongo1: SECONDARY, health 1
- mongo2: PRIMARY, health 1
- mongo3: SECONDARY, health 1
- arbiter: ARBITER, health 1

Conclusion:
A replica set requires a voting majority to maintain its primary.
Without a majority, the primary steps down and cannot accept writes.
After network connectivity was restored, the replica set recovered.
The original four-member Lab 08 configuration was restored.
