OBSERVATION

The non-transactional approach achieved higher throughput
in this experiment because it avoided transaction overhead.

Both approaches maintained the correct stock count in these
tests. However, the non-transactional approach updates stock
and creates an order as separate operations. If order creation
fails after stock is decremented, the data can become inconsistent.

CONCLUSION

Transactions provide stronger consistency and atomicity.
The find_one_and_update approach is faster for this simple
reservation test but does not provide atomicity across both
inventory and order documents.
